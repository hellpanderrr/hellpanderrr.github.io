Setting up — one-time download

The macronizer runs entirely in your browser: it needs the POS-tagger model, the Morpheus morphology engine and an 812,000-form wordlist — about 10 MB to download (65 MB once unpacked). This happens once; afterwards it is stored in your browser, so every later visit starts in a few seconds and works offline. What is this tool?
